🎥 Who's on camera?

The vertical video shows one character at a time. Character 1 stands on the left side of the background, Character 2 on the right — the background slides with each cut. Whoever is off camera keeps talking as a voice-over.

Picking a shot switches to manual and keeps the current cuts as a starting point — handy for holding on a listener's reaction or cutting away mid-sentence.
